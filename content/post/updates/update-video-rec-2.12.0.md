# Video REC v2.12.0
---

We are pleased to announce that our new **Video REC v2.12.0** has been released for suitable production services. This new version improves the recordings list with sequence-number ordering and a clearer visual grouping of related recordings. All users with previous maintained versions can upgrade today. Please contact our support!
