# Report: The CX Gap in Assisted Service
---

Companies have digitalized the transaction, but not the advice. When the customer's need is simple, automation solves it end-to-end — no friction, no wait. When it's complex, though, no one is available with the right expertise, at the right time. That gap is where CX quietly breaks down.

We put numbers behind it: 24 pages, eight documented problems backed by analyst data, and a sector intensity matrix covering banking, insurance, public sector, telco, retail, health, and education. A few of the findings:

→ 📉 **73%** would abandon a purchase over a wait of more than 5 minutes.

→ 📈 **+33%** higher conversion in an assisted sale with video vs. voice.

→ 🚪 **46%** of customers abandon self-service due to lack of information.

None of these are edge cases — they're the default outcome when "digital" stops at the transaction and never reaches the advice a complex moment actually needs.
