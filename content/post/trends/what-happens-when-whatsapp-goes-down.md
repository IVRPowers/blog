# What Happens When WhatsApp Goes Down and It's Your Only Channel?
---

On Monday, August 3rd, WhatsApp went down for hours. For many businesses, that meant losing their only way to reach customers: no orders, no confirmations, no appointment reminders, no replies. It wasn't the first outage, and it won't be the last.

You breathe out because it was only a day, only a few hours. But the real problem was never technical. It's that this channel isn't yours — you don't control its roadmap, its uptime, or its pricing — and it keeps absorbing more and more of your relationship with your customers.

None of this is an argument against WhatsApp as a channel — it's an argument against building your entire customer relationship on a single channel you don't own. Voice, video, and IVR infrastructure that a company controls directly doesn't go dark because a third-party platform has an outage, and it doesn't change its terms overnight because someone else decided to.

🤔 **If your primary customer channel disappeared tomorrow for a full business day, how many of your customers could still reach you?**
